This document describes the design language for our application.

## Theme Overview
The design theme is currently set to `dark` mode, utilizing a modern aesthetic defined by specific fonts, moderate roundedness, and balanced spacing.

## Color Palette
The primary color is `#6750A4`, a deep purple, providing a strong brand identity and focus for interactive elements.
The secondary color is `#FFD700`, a bright gold, used for accents and to complement the primary color.
The neutral color is `#1C1B1F`, a very dark grey, forming the base for backgrounds and surfaces in dark mode.

## Typography
Headlines use the `epilogue` font, providing a distinct and impactful presence.
Body text and labels use the `manrope` font, chosen for its readability and clean appearance across various scales.

## Shape and Form
UI elements feature a `moderate` level of roundedness (value `2`), offering a friendly yet professional feel.

## Spacing
The design employs `normal` spacing (value `2`), ensuring a clear hierarchy and comfortable user experience without feeling too dense or sparse.
